ABOUT US
Designing Spaces With Purpose
MIKO Creations is a Kampala-based interior and landscape design studio dedicated to
creating timeless, functional, and sustainable spaces across residential, commercial,
hospitality, healthcare, retail, and workplace environments.
Our work brings together creative design, technical expertise, project coordination,
production, and execution to transform ideas into thoughtfully crafted spaces.
We believe exceptional design goes beyond aesthetics. A well-designed space should support
the people who use it, improve daily experiences, and continue adding value long after the
project is complete.Every project begins with understanding our clients—their lifestyle, business, aspirations,
and the way they intend to use their spaces. Those insights become the foundation for
environments that feel authentic, balanced, purposeful, and distinctly their own.
Miko Creations, we don't simply design spaces.
We design experiences that people remember.
OUR MISSION
To design thoughtful, sustainable, and timeless spaces that inspire connection, evoke
emotion, and enhance the way people live and work.
OUR VISION
To become a leading force in redefining interior and landscape design across Africa by
setting the standard for authenticity, innovation, quality, and human-centred design.
OUR DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
Everything we create is guided by five principles.
People
We begin by understanding the people behind every project and designing around the way
they live, work, and interact with their environment.
Purpose
Every material, layout, finish, and detail should contribute to the functionality and overall
experience of the space.
Nature
We believe meaningful connections between built environments and the natural world create
healthier, more inspiring spaces.Timelessness
We design beyond trends, creating environments that remain relevant and beautiful for years
to come.
Execution
A strong concept only becomes a successful project through careful planning, skilled
craftsmanship, and attention to every detail.

HOW WE WORK
A Collaborative Process From Concept to Completion
Every successful project begins with understanding.
Our process is structured to ensure clear communication, thoughtful decision-making, and
exceptional execution from the first conversation through final handover.
01
Consultation
We begin by understanding your project, lifestyle, goals, budget, and vision while discussing
how we can best support you.
02Discovery & Site Visit
We assess the site, take measurements, identify opportunities, and gather the information
needed to guide the design process.
03
Concept Development
Layouts, mood boards, space planning, and creative direction are developed to establish the
design vision.
04
Design Development
Detailed drawings, finishes, materials, lighting, furniture selections, and technical
documentation are prepared.
05
Procurement & Execution
We coordinate suppliers, contractors, production, and site implementation while maintaining
quality throughout the project.
06
Styling & Handover
The finishing touches complete the space before the final walkthrough and project handover.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Do you offer consultations?
Yes. Every project begins with a consultation where we discuss your vision, requirements,
budget, and project scope.
Do you only work in Kampala?
No. We undertake projects across Uganda and are open to opportunities throughout East
Africa.
Can you manage both design and execution?
Yes. We provide end-to-end project support, from concept development and technical
drawings to procurement, project management, and execution.
How long does a project take?
Timelines vary depending on the project's size and complexity. A detailed programme is
shared once the project scope has been established.Can you work with my existing contractor?
Absolutely. We are happy to collaborate with contractors, consultants, and suppliers already
involved in your project.
Do you customise every design?
Yes. Every project is designed specifically around the client's needs, lifestyle, brand, and
functional requirements.
