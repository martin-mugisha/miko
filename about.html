About MIKO

Crafting environments with intentional elegance.

We are a Kampala-based interior and landscape design studio creating timeless, functional, and sustainable spaces.

Across homes, workplaces, hospitality, healthcare, retail, and outdoor environments, we bring creative design, technical expertise, project coordination, and careful execution together.

Every project starts with understanding the people behind it—their lifestyle, business, aspirations, and the way they want to use their space.

“We don't simply design spaces. We design experiences people remember.”

Our Mission 01

Thoughtful spaces that enrich everyday life.

To design sustainable, timeless spaces that inspire connection, evoke emotion, and enhance the way people live and work.

Our Vision 02

Setting a new standard for design across Africa.

To become a leading force in interior and landscape design through authenticity, innovation, quality, and human-centred thinking.
